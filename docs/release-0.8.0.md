# ParaNAM Trainer 0.8.0

## What's new since 0.7.6

- **New capture sampling methods:** scrambled **Sobol** (the default for new projects) and **Optimized LHS** join Standard, Maximin, Orthogonal and Near-orthogonal LHS. Sampling remains seed-reproducible; existing saved plans retain their selected method.
- **Revised capture recommendations:** lower Sobol training budgets (32 training / 8 validation captures for 2–3 knobs, 64 / 16 for 4–6 knobs, with further scaling for additional controls). For all sampling methods, the suggested validation count is 25% of the training count, automatically updated when the training count changes and still editable independently. Available grid positions and reserved captures can limit the totals.
- **Improved switch sampling:** newly added switches default to Simple. Sobol distributes Simple Switch states within a shared training budget and samples Complex Switch combinations separately, with reproducible coverage.
- **Optional separate default capture:** the new **Include default as additional capture** option is enabled by default. It adds one default-setting training capture outside the sampled training count, without forcing the sampled design to contain that point. Existing saved plans are not regenerated silently.
- **Unified Rules:** the former Avoid Rules area is now **Rules**, where each rule can be **Avoid** or **Mute**. Numeric conditions now support strict `<` and `>`, inclusive `≤` and `≥`, or `=`; switches use `=`. Legacy knob-local Avoid thresholds are migrated to equivalent Rules in editable projects, without changing frozen training snapshots.
- **Mark captures as Mute or Unstable:** classifications stay visible in Capture under **Tags/Warnings** with blue labels. Unstable positions are excluded and replaced by valid points; Mute settings instead use synthetic silent recordings and need no physical recording.
- **Synthetic Mute Captures:** silent knob endpoints and Mute combinations use normal full-length zero-output WAV targets in the capture plan. Replanning regenerates all required synthetic Mute rows and discards obsolete Mute corners that are not part of the new plan; no second network or player-format change is needed.
- **Mute at minimum and Mute at maximum:** mark silent endpoints and automatically generate silent synthetic capture rows at those exact settings, including on legacy projects with nearby shifted physical corners. Existing projects do not have these options enabled automatically.
- **Legacy Plan and Compare fixes:** old projects no longer repeatedly request Plan updates because a validation capture is synthetic Mute, and training can be added once every required capture is complete. The Compare control panel remains visible even when no model controls are present.
- **Linear Volume captures:** other numeric controls are held at 10 (or their nearest valid maximum), with switches in their highest positions. Volume sweeps cover the entire volume range regardless of Avoid/Rules restrictions; ordinary training captures keep the Linear Volume control at maximum.
- **Capture-position guidance:** a white segment on each relevant knob arc shows movement from the previous recording to the currently selected setting. It pulses transparently together with the tile highlight, revealing only the red arc at its dimmest point, and stops once **My rig matches…** is confirmed.
- **Per-capture RMS loss normalization:** now enabled by default for new parametric training, with an adjustable exponent from 0.01 to 2 (default 2). It compensates the **MSE** training loss using capture power, a −18 dBFS RMS reference and a −60 dBFS floor; it does not alter the original audio, inference gain or validation metrics.
- **SNR-weighted loss normalization:** also enabled by default and independently selectable. It uses pre-blip noise from the raw amp-return recording, downweighting noisy examples with a capped linear reliability factor for **MSE** (minimum 10%) and a gentler square-root factor for **MRSTFT** (minimum 20%). Recordings without usable raw noise measurements retain a neutral SNR weight rather than an invented estimate.
- **Training-data and checkpoint consistency:** SNR measurements and captured audio are frozen with queue jobs; normalization options are persisted and checked when resuming. Previously saved normalization choices are preserved.
- **Editable epoch targets in Queue:** right-click a queued or completed training job to change its total epoch target. Completed jobs can resume from checkpoints when the target increases; the target cannot be lowered below already completed epochs.
- **Retry failed training jobs:** failed jobs offer **Retry** with checkpoint recovery.
- **Safer Retrain:** Retrain produces an independent, editable snapshot of the original job's recorded capture plan and audio, without adding a duplicate entry to Projects or incorporating later unrecorded active-learning suggestions. The original job and source project remain untouched.
- **Project duplicate naming:** copying a project ending in a numbered suffix now advances that suffix (for example, ` (2)` becomes ` (3)`) instead of nesting suffixes.
- **Interface and runtime fixes:** prevent runaway repeated +/− changes in advanced training numeric fields; support extended Windows paths when extracting optional GPU runtime files.
- **Compatibility:** existing captured projects are not silently replanned. Exported `.pnam` models retain the existing player-facing architecture, control schema and inference requirements.
